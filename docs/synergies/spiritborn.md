# Spiritborn synergy map

Reference for putting Spiritborn builds together so their parts work in concert. Each mechanic lists what **makes it happen** and what **pays it off**. Pick an engine you can keep running, make the main skill feed it, then stack payoffs, preferring [x] over [+]. Read [README.md](README.md) first for the method and the rules that apply to every class.

Made from the planner's Season 15 game data on 22 Nov 2026. `X` is a number that scales with rank or roll. The machine-readable copy is `data/synergy-tags.json`.

## Spiritborn engines

- **Spirit Hall.** Your primary and secondary Spirit Hall choices (Jaguar, Eagle, Gorilla, Centipede) decide which skill tags get the bonuses. Pick payoffs for the tags your main skill carries.
- **Poison (Centipede).** Toxic Skin, Scourge, The Devourer and Stinger poison enemies. Stinger pays off Poisoned enemies, and Aspect of Debilitating Toxins and Disobedience pay off damage over time.
- **Counterattack and Resolve (Gorilla).** Counterattack, Concussive Stomp, The Protector and Rock Splitter make Resolve and Counterattacks. Aspect of Glynn's Anvil pays off Resolve. Counterattack of the Swarm is the meta tower push build.
- **Vigor.** Ravager, Toxic Skin and Vortex give back Vigor. Rod of Kepeleke and the Vigor-cost upgrades pay off spending it.
- **Crowd control.** Concussive Stomp, Scourge, Vortex's Knock Down and The Protector crowd control enemies, which turns on Aspect of Apprehension.
- **Movement.** Toxic Skin pays off movement. Aspect of Fleet Wings, Soar and The Protector's Canopy Shelter make it.
