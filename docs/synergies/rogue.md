# Rogue synergy map

Reference for putting Rogue builds together so their parts work in concert. Each mechanic lists what **makes it happen** and what **pays it off**. Pick an engine you can keep running, make the main skill feed it, then stack payoffs, preferring [x] over [+]. Read [README.md](README.md) first for the method and the rules that apply to every class.

Made from the planner's Season 15 game data on 22 Nov 2026. `X` is a number that scales with rank or roll. The machine-readable copy is `data/synergy-tags.json`.

## Rogue engines

- **Imbuements.** Poison, Cold and Shadow Imbuement upgrade the next skills you cast. Cowl of the Nameless, Etna's Lost Dagger and Aspect of Imitated Imbuement pay off using them. Poison Imbuement turns the main skill into damage over time, so Penetrating Shot and Dance of Knives can use Grief, Blood-Mad Idol and Aspect of Disobedience.
- **Stealth and Concealment.** Concealment, Dark Shroud, Dash's Primer and Smoke Grenade's Silhouette make you Stealthed or Concealed. Shrouded Gift and Aspect of Mending Obscurity pay that off.
- **Combo Points.** Basic skills build Combo Points and Core skills spend them. Look for "per Combo Point spent" payoffs.
- **Traps and Ultimates.** Death Trap and Rain of Arrows builds use Beastfall Boots and Aspect of Aftermath to refund energy and reset the Ultimate.
- **Cold crowd control.** Cold Imbuement and Aspect of Frozen Memories chill and freeze enemies. Cold Imbuement's Buffered Battery pays off Lucky Hits.
- **Movement.** Concealment pays off movement, and Shadow Step and Dash make it.
