# Warlock synergy map

Reference for putting Warlock builds together so their parts work in concert. Each mechanic lists what **makes it happen** and what **pays it off**. Pick an engine you can keep running, make the main skill feed it, then stack payoffs, preferring [x] over [+]. Read [README.md](README.md) first for the method and the rules that apply to every class.

Made from the planner's Season 15 game data on 22 Nov 2026. `X` is a number that scales with rank or roll. The machine-readable copy is `data/synergy-tags.json`.

## Warlock engines

- **Volatility.** Sigil of Chaos (and Infernal Breath through Infernal Homunculus) empowers skills with Volatility. Hellfire skills and Moloch's Beating Flame scale with every skill you have empowered, so keep as many empowered as you can.
- **Demonform and Metamorphosis.** Metamorphosis puts you in Demonform. Its Destruction Demon and Stagger Bar upgrades make Volatility and crowd control.
- **Summons and demons.** Bombardment, the Summon skills and Sigil of Subversion make demons. Misanthropic and Sigil of Summons pay them off.
- **Dark Prison.** Dark Prison groups enemies together, crowd controls them and can Fortify you. It is on almost every meta bar.
- **Life stacking.** Blazing Scream builds stack Maximum Life because Volatility costs Life and several powers scale with it. Temerity's Barrier pays off Undying.
- **Hex and Sigils.** Hexed enemies and Sigil skills have their own payoffs. Check the lists below.
