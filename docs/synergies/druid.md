# Druid synergy map

Reference for putting Druid builds together so their parts work in concert. Each mechanic lists what **makes it happen** and what **pays it off**. Pick an engine you can keep running, make the main skill feed it, then stack payoffs, preferring [x] over [+]. Read [README.md](README.md) first for the method and the rules that apply to every class.

Made from the planner's Season 15 game data on 22 Nov 2026. `X` is a number that scales with rank or roll. The machine-readable copy is `data/synergy-tags.json`.

## Druid engines

- **Shapeshifting.** Werewolf and Werebear skills shift you into that form. Grizzly Rage keeps you in Werebear form and Fortifies you. Pick payoffs for the form your main skill uses.
- **Storm and Earth.** Lightning Storm, Tornado, Hurricane, Boulder and Landslide are Storm or Earth skills. Fractured Runestone and Overcharged pay off casting them. Werenado mixes both by turning Tornado into a Werewolf skill.
- **Poison and damage over time.** Lacerate, Rabies, Poison Creeper and Stone Burst add damage over time. That turns on Aspect of Debilitating Toxins, Aspect of the Dark Howl and Aspect of Disobedience (Damage Reduction on every tick). Rabies meta builds use Disobedience.
- **Fortify.** Earthen Bulwark and Grizzly Rage's Fortify upgrade keep you Fortified, which turns on Crushing and Conceited.
- **Companions.** Wolves, Ravens and Poison Creeper are Companions. The Companion builds stack the "your Companions" payoffs.
- **Crowd control.** Debilitating Roar, Petrify and Frostburn crowd control enemies. Azurewrath and The Basilisk pay off Frozen or Petrified enemies.
