# Necromancer synergy map

Reference for putting Necromancer builds together so their parts work in concert. Each mechanic lists what **makes it happen** and what **pays it off**. Pick an engine you can keep running, make the main skill feed it, then stack payoffs, preferring [x] over [+]. Read [README.md](README.md) first for the method and the rules that apply to every class.

Made from the planner's Season 15 game data on 22 Nov 2026. `X` is a number that scales with rank or roll. The machine-readable copy is `data/synergy-tags.json`.

## Necromancer engines

- **Minions.** Skeleton Warriors, Mages, Golem and Army of the Dead. Pick the "your Minions" payoffs and the Skeleton Mage and Warrior upgrades that buff minion damage.
- **Corpses.** Sever, Corpse Tendrils and Corpse Explosion upgrades make Corpses. Corpse Explosion and Bone Storm builds spend them.
- **Blood and Overpower.** Blood Wave, Blood Lance and Blood Mist work with Blood Orbs and Overpower. Blood Wave is the strongest Necromancer build this season.
- **Essence spending.** Iron Maiden and Corpse Tendrils give back Essence. Bone Spirit's Maximum Essence and Core Skill upgrades pay off a big Essence pool.
- **Crit and block (borrowed from other classes).** The Bone Spirit and Army builds wear Aspect of Redirected Force (crit damage from Block Chance) with Aspect of Interdiction. Block Chance pays off outside Paladin too.
- **Curses and crowd control.** Decrepify, Bone Prison and Corpse Tendrils crowd control enemies. That turns on Will of Rathma, Aspect of Dominance and the Skeleton Mage crowd control upgrades.
- **Damage over time.** Blight, Shadowblight, The Gloom Ward and Blood-Mad Idol feed the damage-over-time payoffs. Disobedience gives Damage Reduction here too.
