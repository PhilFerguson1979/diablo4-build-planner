# Sorcerer synergy map

Reference for putting Sorcerer builds together so their parts work in concert. Each mechanic lists what **makes it happen** and what **pays it off**. Pick an engine you can keep running, make the main skill feed it, then stack payoffs, preferring [x] over [+]. Read [README.md](README.md) first for the method and the rules that apply to every class.

Made from the planner's Season 15 game data on 22 Nov 2026. `X` is a number that scales with rank or roll. The machine-readable copy is `data/synergy-tags.json`.

## Sorcerer engines

- **Burning.** Firewall, Ring of Fire, Inferno, Fire Bolt and Meteor keep enemies Burning. Overheating and the Burning payoffs scale it. Firewall is the strongest Sorcerer build this season. Burning is damage over time, so Aspect of Disobedience gives Damage Reduction off it (Firewall meta builds use it).
- **Cold.** Blizzard, Frozen Orb, Ice Spikes, Deep Freeze and the Frigid Hydra chill and freeze enemies. Sunderfrost, Aspect of Biting Cold and Deep Freeze's Vulnerable upgrade pay off Frozen and Vulnerable enemies.
- **Conjurations and Enchantments.** Hydra, Ice Blades, Lightning Spear and Familiar are Conjurations. Enchantment slots add a second skill's passive effect.
- **Crackling Energy.** Ball Lightning, Chain Lightning and Unstable Currents make and collect Crackling Energy. Unstable Currents' Boundless pays it off.
- **Barriers.** Frozen Orb's and Blizzard's Barrier upgrades make Barriers, which pay off Aspect of the Untarnished Blaze and Conceited.
