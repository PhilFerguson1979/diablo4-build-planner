# Argent Veil, Divine Lance with Grief (Paladin)

*Made by the build agent on 2026-11-24. Everything below comes from the planner's maths and has not been checked in game.*

## The idea

The tanky version of the Argent Veil Divine Lance build: Grief in the main hand and Herald of Zakarum in the off-hand instead of The Grandfather, which moves into the Talisman. Grief's 40% poison rides on every Divine Lance hit and its +750 Weapon Damage adds to every hit; the shield adds block, armor and resistances.

## How it works

- **Crowd control:** made by Aegis, Condemn and Control; paid off by Vanquishing, Exploiter's and Control.
- **Evade and Dodge:** made by Juggernaut's and Argent Veil; paid off by Juggernaut's.
- **Damage over time:** made by Blood-Mad Idol and Grief; paid off by Blood-Mad Idol.
- **Movement:** made by Exploiter's, Defiance Aura, Arbiter of Justice > Movement Speed, Condemn and Condemn > Movement Speed; paid off by Aspect of Inner Calm, Argent Veil and Condemn.
- **Vulnerable:** made by Fanaticism Aura > Rite of Humility, Arbiter of Justice > Seraph's Wings and Condemn > Gather the Guilty; paid off by Fanaticism Aura > Rite of Humility and Apostle .
- **Arbiter form:** made by Arbiter of Justice > Seraph's Wings, Arbiter of Justice > Wing Strike Recast and Arbiter of Justice > Movement Speed; paid off by Arbiter of Justice > Seraph's Wings, Arbiter of Justice > Wing Strike Recast and Arbiter of Justice > Movement Speed.
- **Critical Strikes:** made by Fanaticism Aura and Locran's Talisman; paid off by The Grandfather, Locran's Talisman and Spirit.

## Rotation

- **Condemn:** cast on cooldown (every 10.5 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Arbiter of Justice:** Ultimate. Cast it on cooldown (every 108 seconds after Cooldown Reduction), ideally when elites or the boss are in range.
- **Fanaticism Aura:** Aura. Its passive stays on while it is on your bar. Cast it on cooldown for the active effect (every 13.5 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Defiance Aura:** Aura. Its passive stays on while it is on your bar. Cast it on cooldown for the active effect (every 18 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Aegis:** cast on cooldown (every 14.4 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Divine Lance:** your main skill. Cast it as much as you can.
