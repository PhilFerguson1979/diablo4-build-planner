## How it works

- **Damage over time:** made by Blood-Mad Idol and Grief; paid off by Aspect of Disobedience and Blood-Mad Idol.
- **Cooldowns:** made by Holy Light Aura > Rite of Judgement, Purify > Sentence and Purify > Cooldown Reduction; paid off by Rakanoth's Wake.
- **Crowd control:** made by Fortress > Rampart of Thorns, Condemn and Spear of the Heavens; paid off by Vanquishing.
- **Movement:** made by Defiance Aura, Fortress > Unstoppable and Condemn; paid off by Aspect of Inner Calm and Condemn.
- **Critical Strikes:** made by Fanaticism Aura; paid off by Fanaticism Aura > Rite of Vengeance and Spirit.
- **Resolve (Juggernaut):** made by Defiance Aura > Rite of Might; paid off by Fortress.
- **Thorns:** made by Fortress > Rampart of Thorns and Revenge; paid off by Fortress > Rampart of Thorns and Revenge.
- **Judgement:** made by Law; paid off by Spear of the Heavens > Cooldown Reduction and Law.

## Rotation

- **Purify:** cast on cooldown (every 6.88 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Holy Light Aura:** Aura. Its passive stays on while it is on your bar. Cast it on cooldown for the active effect (every 13.22 seconds after Cooldown Reduction). This is the main damage.
- **Fanaticism Aura:** Aura. Its passive stays on while it is on your bar. Cast it on cooldown for the active effect (every 12.9 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Defiance Aura:** Aura. Its passive stays on while it is on your bar. Cast it on cooldown for the active effect (every 17.2 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Spear of the Heavens:** cast on cooldown (every 12.04 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Fortress:** Ultimate. Cast it on cooldown (every 51.6 seconds after Cooldown Reduction), ideally when elites or the boss are in range.
