## The idea

Brandish is a Disciple Basic Skill, so the Light's Epiphany 5-piece multiplies it by 500%[x] during Arbiter of Justice, the same engine as Divine Lance. Supplication makes it fire in all directions for 120 to 160%[x] more damage, and on a big boss several arcs can land at once.

## How it works

- **Crowd control:** made by Aegis, Condemn and Control; paid off by Exploiter's, Vanquishing and Control.
- **Critical Strikes:** made by Fanaticism Aura and Locran's Talisman; paid off by The Grandfather and Locran's Talisman.
- **Resource spending:** made by Brandish; paid off by Fanaticism Aura.
- **Vulnerable:** made by Fanaticism Aura > Rite of Humility, Arbiter of Justice > Seraph's Wings and Condemn > Gather the Guilty; paid off by Fanaticism Aura > Rite of Humility and Apostle .
- **Arbiter form:** made by Arbiter of Justice > Seraph's Wings, Arbiter of Justice > Wing Strike Recast, Arbiter of Justice > Movement Speed and Arbiter; paid off by Arbiter of Justice > Seraph's Wings, Arbiter of Justice > Wing Strike Recast, Arbiter of Justice > Movement Speed and Arbiter.
- **Movement:** made by Exploiter's, Defiance Aura, Arbiter of Justice > Movement Speed, Condemn and Condemn > Movement Speed; paid off by Condemn.

## Rotation

- **Condemn:** cast on cooldown (every 10.5 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Arbiter of Justice:** Ultimate. Cast it on cooldown (every 108 seconds after Cooldown Reduction), ideally when elites or the boss are in range.
- **Fanaticism Aura:** Aura. Its passive stays on while it is on your bar. Cast it on cooldown for the active effect (every 13.5 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Defiance Aura:** Aura. Its passive stays on while it is on your bar. Cast it on cooldown for the active effect (every 18 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Aegis:** cast on cooldown (every 14.4 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Brandish:** your main skill. Cast it as much as you can.
