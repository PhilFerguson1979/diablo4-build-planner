# Fist of the Heavens, Advance and Judgement (Paladin)

*Made by the build agent on 2026-11-23. Everything below comes from the planner's maths and has not been checked in game.*

## The idea

Fist of the Heavens turns Spear of the Heavens into a Core spender. Advance is the Faith generator and keeps the Paladin moving, so Relentless stays on honestly. Aegis Faith Is My Shield and Heaven's Radiant Fire keep Judgement up, and Spear's Judgement Damage Bonus is a pure [x] against Judged enemies. Inner Calm and Vanquishing are banned because their conditions clash with moving and with bosses.

## How it works

- **Damage over time:** made by Blood-Mad Idol; paid off by Aspect of Disobedience and Blood-Mad Idol.
- **Crowd control:** made by Holy Light Aura > Rite of Submission, Aegis, Aegis > Faith Is My Shield, Spear of the Heavens and Control; paid off by Exploiter's, Vanquishing and Control.
- **Judgement:** made by Aegis > Faith Is My Shield and Law; paid off by Cathedral's Song, Spear of the Heavens > Cooldown Reduction, Spear of the Heavens > Judgement Damage Bonus and Law.
- **Block:** made by Aegis; paid off by Aegis > Faith Is My Shield.
- **Resource spending:** made by Advance; paid off by Fanaticism Aura.
- **Critical Strikes:** made by Fanaticism Aura and Advance > Critical Strike Chance; paid off by Fanaticism Aura > Rite of Vengeance.
- **Movement:** made by Tibault's Will, Exploiter's, Defiance Aura and Advance > Unhindered; paid off by Advance > Unhindered.

## Rotation

- **Spear of the Heavens:** your main skill. Cast it as much as you can.
- **Advance:** spends resource. Cast it between Spear of the Heavens casts when you have resource to spare, or keep it up if it summons or lingers.
- **Holy Light Aura:** Aura. Its passive stays on while it is on your bar. Cast it on cooldown for the active effect (every 21.5 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Fanaticism Aura:** Aura. Its passive stays on while it is on your bar. Cast it on cooldown for the active effect (every 12.9 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Defiance Aura:** Aura. Its passive stays on while it is on your bar. Cast it on cooldown for the active effect (every 17.2 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
- **Aegis:** cast on cooldown (every 13.76 seconds after Cooldown Reduction).
